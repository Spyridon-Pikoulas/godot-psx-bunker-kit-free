# PSX Bunker Kit Free

**15 free modular PS1-style pieces to build a fallout shelter's dormitory** for your survival horror game: painted concrete floor and ceiling, board-marked walls plain and stencilled B-2, the bulkhead doorway and its door whose wheel turns before it swings, steel bunks, lockers that open, a gas mask rack, supply shelves, a water drum, an extract fan that turns, a bulkhead light and a civil defence poster. They snap to a 2 m grid. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per piece with its colliders, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### What's inside

- **Structure:** a painted concrete floor and ceiling, a board-marked wall plain and stencilled
  B-2, the bulkhead doorway and a corner post, on a 2 m grid, 3 m high.
- **The bulkhead door:** its wheel turns, then it swings, its colliders moving with it.
- **The dormitory:** steel bunk beds, a steel locker whose door opens on a coat, a gas mask rack,
  supply shelves, a water drum.
- **Fittings:** an extract fan that turns, a bulkhead light, a civil defence poster.
- **3 animations** on the moving parts, as clips in GLB and takes in FBX.
- **15 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, and the seamless floor, wall and ceiling
  textures as their own PNGs.
- One 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours, real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per piece: box colliders on every part (a doorway's leave the way through
clear), an AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your flashlight falls on it, and
a gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# door: the piece in your level
door.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the kit (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.

For the sound of it, [PSX Horror SFX](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx) has footsteps
on concrete and metal, creaking doors, creatures, stingers and the typewriter save, PS1-style
([30 free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx-free)).
